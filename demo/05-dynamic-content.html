05 — Dynamic content

Simulates infinite scroll / lazily-loaded content to exercise the MutationObserver path in content.js.

Steps: 1) toggle the extension ON first (this initial paragraph is the page's only candidate, so the notice's progress bar reads "1 / 1" and the line under it reads "1 simplified · 0 unchanged" or "0 simplified · 1 unchanged" depending on whether the model actually changed the wording). 2) Click "Load more content" a few times — one template per click, cycling through four. Expected: each click's new items are processed automatically without re-toggling, and the notice's counts are cumulative across clicks (the bar's total climbs and the skipped line's "x / y" grows with it), not reset each time — the running totals are kept across observer batches rather than reset per batch — see the foundTotal/sentCount comments at the top of extension/content.js. The 4th template ("OK") is under the 4-word minimum, so it always lands in the muted "skipped (e.g. too short)" line without ever calling the API — the bar's total counts only items that actually got a request, and "simplified" is the subset of those whose text came back different from what was sent. 3) Click "Remove oldest loaded item" a few times. Expected: the totals decrease to match, in whichever line that item had landed in (the bar's count, the simplified/unchanged split, or the skipped line) — simulating a virtualized/infinite-scroll list recycling old rows out of the DOM.

This initial paragraph exists before any dynamic content is loaded, verbosely stated for the purpose of demonstration.
