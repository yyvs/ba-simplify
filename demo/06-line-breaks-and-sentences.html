06 — Line breaks & sentence splitting

Checks the two-stage split: <br> boundaries are cut in the extension (collectChunkNodes()) before anything is sent, and each resulting chunk is the sentence-split in the backend (split_sentences(), pysbd) so every sentence reaches the model on its own. Watch the progress notice and the backend log to see how many requests each case produces.

Expected: one request per sentence — 3 total. A multi-sentence block is split in the backend, each sentence simplified independently, then rejoined. Inline links and bold are flattened for the request only: the links and the bold are still in the page afterwards, holding whatever of their own text the model kept.

The Computer Game Designers Symposium, later known as the Computer Game Developers Conference, was started in 1988 by Chris Crawford. He had already been producing a bimonthly newsletter for game developers, The Journal of Computer Game Design, since 1987. One of the subscribers, game programmer Nicky Robinson, suggested that he hold a gathering.

Expected: 3 chunks, cut at the <br>s, and the line breaks are still there after simplifying. Critically, the first two facts must not be merged into one sentence — they have no punctuation between them, so only the <br> keeps them apart. The third chunk contains two sentences and is additionally sentence-split in the backend, so this case produces 4 requests in total.

they were founded in 1988
the headquarters is in San Jose
the themesong changed 5 times. the original one is now forbidden.

Expected: words stay separated. Before the <br> fix a <br> contributed zero characters to textContent, so this used to flatten into "…acknowledgmentAll participating…" with the two words fused together.

Prior to the commencement of the undertaking, stakeholders must furnish written acknowledgment
All participating entities are required to comply with the terms herein described.

Expected: the blank line between the two blocks survives — a <br><br> gap is kept as an empty chunk rather than collapsed away.

The initial phase of the aforementioned procedure commences imminently.

Subsequent phases shall be enumerated in a separate communication.

Expected: not split after "Dr." or "U.S." — pysbd handles abbreviations, which a naive split on ". " would get wrong. 2 requests, not 4.

Dr. Smith relocated to the U.S. in 1998 to commence his research. He subsequently characterised the experience as transformative.

Expected: only the prose chunk is sent. The short chunk ("Home page") fails the 4-word minimum and the URL chunk is skipped outright, but both keep their place between the line breaks after simplifying.

Home page
Despite the multiplicity of antecedent conditions, the regulatory framework mandates uniform compliance.
see https://example.com/terms for details

Expected: toggling off restores this exact markup — the <br>s, the link, and the bold text all come back, since revert replays the original HTML rather than the flattened text.

Pursuant to the aforementioned stipulations, all parties shall comply.
