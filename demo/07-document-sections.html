Only meaninwgful with the document model selected (right-click the toolbar icon → Simplifier model → "Custom fine-tuned document model"). That model is trained on whole D-Wikipedia articles, so the extension collects text differently here: it finds the main content area, cuts it into heading-delimited sections, and sends each section as one document. With any of the three sentence-granularity models this page behaves like a normal page instead — a good A/B comparison.

Expected, in document mode: headings stay exactly as they are (they act as dividers and are never sent), and each section still has every paragraph it started with — the answer is divided back across them. The model deletes, merges and reorders sentences, so its output has no position-by-position correspondence to its input: a sentence can land in the paragraph next to the one it came from, and a paragraph the answer had nothing left for keeps its own text. What must not happen is a paragraph, list item or link disappearing. Toggling off restores every one of them exactly.

Formation

The International Game Developers Association is incorporated in the United States as a not-for-profit organization. It has over twelve thousand members drawn from all fields of game development, including programming, visual art, audio engineering and production management.

In recognition of the wide-ranging, multidisciplinary nature of contemporary game development, everyone who participates in any capacity within the game development process is welcome to apply for membership of the association.

Early conferences

The Computer Game Designers Symposium, subsequently known as the Computer Game Developers Conference, was inaugurated in 1988. Its founder had previously been producing a bimonthly newsletter for game developers since 1987.

Subsequent expansion

Throughout the ensuing decade the organization broadened its remit considerably, establishing regional chapters across multiple continents and instituting a series of special interest groups devoted to particular disciplines within the industry.

Contemporary commentators have observed that this period of institutional consolidation coincided with a substantial diversification of the commercial landscape in which its membership operated.
