Extension Demo Pages

  • 05 — Dynamic content: MutationObserver / infinite-scroll-style insertion.
  • 06 — Line breaks & sentences: <br> chunking and per-sentence simplification.
  • 07 — Document sections: heading-delimited sections for the document model.
  • 08 — HTML checker: paste markup, rendered twice side by side — pinned original vs. simplified copy.
